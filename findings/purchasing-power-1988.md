# What 58 to 90 roubles were worth in 1988

Context for the amounts on the STIPENDIYA ведомость of SPTU-132, October
1988, computed and printed by `emulator/iskra_run.py`.

## The amounts from the run

| Student | practical wage | stipend | deductions, meals | net, К ВЫДАЧЕ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Иванов И.И.* | 46.80 | 30.00 | 12.40 | **64.40** |
| Петров П.П.* | 39.50 | 30.00 | 11.20 | **58.30** |
| Сидорова А.А.* | 58.20 | 45.00 | 13.60 | **89.60** |
| **total** | 144.50 | 105.00 | 37.20 | **212.30** |

The asterisk in a name is the software's full-month marker. Without it the
proportional formula applies, `V84 = days worked / days in the month`.

## The Soviet wage scale in 1988

| Position | roubles per month |
|---|---|
| minimum wage | 70 |
| factory entrant | 90 to 120 |
| engineer | 130 to 180 |
| **average wage** | **about 220** |

The three students, at 58 to 90 roubles net, sat at a quarter to two fifths
of the average wage. Сидорова at 89.60 was effectively at factory-entrant
level, as a vocational student.

## Why the stipend alone was so thin

The base stipend of 30 roubles, raised to 45, was deliberately set at
subsistence, because ПТУ students also received:

- a place in the dormitory, free or nominal
- subsidised canteen meals, which is what the ПИТАНИЕ columns on the form
  are for, and where the deductions of about 12 roubles come from
- often working clothes

The real money was the practical placement. During производственная
практика the students worked in an enterprise and received a share of what
they produced, which is the ЗАРПЛАТА column with its 39 to 58 roubles.
That is exactly what the asterisk and the proportional logic in the software
exist for.

## What that bought

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| bread | 16 to 20 kopeks |
| 1 l milk | about 30 kopeks |
| 1 kg sausage | 2.20 to 2.90 roubles |
| cinema ticket | 25 to 50 kopeks |
| bus fare | 5 kopeks |
| cassette recorder | 150 to 300 roubles |
| jeans, black market | 100 to 150 roubles |
| Zhiguli, a car | 7,000 to 9,000 roubles |

For a teenager whose lodging and half of whose food were provided anyway, 60
to 90 roubles a month meant real spending power. A cassette recorder was two
or three good placement months away, and so were the jeans. Above that the
scale closed: no number of good months moved the car.

## The point about the software

That a vocational school in 1988 used an Iskra-226, a machine worth tens of
thousands of roubles, to reconcile a few dozen amounts between 30 and 90
roubles says what payroll accounting was worth. Every kopek of the total
line, 212.30, had to match against the cash desk, signed off by the director
and the chief accountant. That is exactly the signature ceremony printed at
the bottom of the ведомость:

```
ИТОГО К ВЫДАЧЕ : ... РУБ. ... КОП.
ДИРЕКТОР:            ВЕДОМОСТЬ СОСТАВИЛ:
ВЫДАЧА ПРОИЗВЕДЕНА В ПРИСУТСТВИИ МАСТЕРА:
ВЫДАЧУ ПРОИЗВЕЛ:
```
