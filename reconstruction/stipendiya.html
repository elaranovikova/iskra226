  An interactive reconstruction of the program «СТИПЕНДИЯ»
  (Gorenburgov M.A., Vintskevich V.V., Muchkina L.V., SPTU-132, version 01.09.88 /
  04.04.89), decoded from the original disk images (oldpc.su/880.rar) of an
  Iskra-226. Screen text, menu structure, branch logic and operating rituals
  (write protect! CONTINUE!) follow the reconstructed BASIC-02 listing. Menu
  entries marked with * are inferred from the ON-GOTO structure, because
  their PRINT lines are damaged in the image. The payout arithmetic is a
  simplified stand-in.
